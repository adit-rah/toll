Privacy Policy

Toll · toll.alders.dev · Last updated: [date of sign-off]

In short

  • Without an account, Toll never contacts our servers. Everything stays on your phone.
  • With an account, we store what Toll records at the gate (when you hit a block, waited, or passed), not what you do in your apps.
  • Toll cannot see which apps you block, your screen time, or anything inside your apps. Apple does not give apps that information.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Toll is free and never asks for money.

Using Toll without an account

Blocks, waits, passes, your history and your own insights are stored only on your phone. Toll makes no network requests for them. If you later create an account, the history on your phone is uploaded to it, and Toll tells you before it does.

What we store when you have an account

  • Account: your display name, your @handle and, if you add one, your photo (stored as a small JPEG with its metadata removed).
  • Sign-in: if you sign in with email, your email address, kept only to send sign-in codes and to recover your account. If you use Sign in with Apple, the email Apple gives us (often a private relay address) and a token that lets us revoke the sign-in when you delete your account. We find your account using one-way hashes of your Apple identifier and email.
  • Gate events: the type of event (block hit, wait started, finished or cancelled, pass, session ended, block added, changed or removed, daily check-in), its time and time-zone offset, wait and session lengths, and random identifiers for each app and block. These identifiers are created on your phone and do not reveal which app they stand for.
  • App names you choose: the name you pick for each blocked app from a fixed list (for example "Instagram"), and each block's schedule. Block names you type stay on your phone.
  • People: your friends, friend requests, people you blocked, and reports you send.
  • School: if you verify a school, which school, and a one-way hash of the school email (not the address).
  • Exam weeks you mark.
  • Devices: a random device identifier, the push notification token, and your time zone.

Contacts

If you choose to find friends from your contacts, your phone turns each email address and phone number into a salted one-way hash and sends only those hashes. We compare them with accounts that allow being found and do not keep them. To let others find you, we store a salted hash of your own email. You can turn "Let people find me" off in Settings.

Who sees what

  • Friends (both of you agreed) see your passes this week and last week, trend, clean days, today's count and status, your blocked app names with their schedules, and your passes today and yesterday. They see when you remove or loosen a block, and when Toll has been off on your phone.
  • Friends of your friends see your name, handle, photo, status and trend. Never app names or passes.
  • People at your school see your display name and handle. School numbers are totals, shown only when enough members are active.
  • Anyone you block, and anyone who blocks you, sees nothing of you and you see nothing of them.

How we use it

Only to run Toll: showing you and your friends what happened at the gate, the daily summary notification if you leave it on, school totals, and insights. We count, without any identifier, how many people use Toll (TelemetryDeck) and collect crash reports (Sentry) to fix problems.

Service providers

Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Resend (sending sign-in emails), Apple (Sign in with Apple and push notifications), Sentry (error reports) and TelemetryDeck (anonymous usage counts). They process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Individual gate events are deleted after 90 days. Daily and weekly totals are kept while your account exists, so your history and trends keep working. Backups are kept for at most 90 days.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete account. After you confirm with Apple or an email code, we revoke Sign in with Apple, delete your account and everything linked to it, remove your photo, and email a confirmation if we have your email. Toll then signs out and wipes its data on your phone. We keep a one-way hash of your Apple identifier so old friend connections cannot be brought back; it cannot be turned back into your identity. Reports you made about others are kept without your account.

Age

Toll is for people 16 and older.
