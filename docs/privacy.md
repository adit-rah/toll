# Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

**Dusk collects no data.** Dusk has no servers, and nothing you do in the app is sent to us. There are no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting, and there is no third-party code in the app. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Your blocks:** their hours, the apps and sites each one blocks, your unlocks and your trial start. These are stored on your iPhone and are deleted with the app. The trial start date stays in your iPhone's Keychain, so reinstalling does not start the free nights again.
- **Places.** For each block with a place (home, or another place such as a library), Dusk stores the point you confirm and a circle around it. iOS tells the app only whether you are inside a circle, and the app keeps that as a yes or no. It never records where you go, and it stores no coordinates other than those points.
- **Screen Time.** Apple's Screen Time framework does the blocking. The app never learns which apps you use, how long you use them, or the names of the apps you choose: it sees only opaque tokens that iOS gives it, and they never leave your iPhone.
- **A log** of when the block turned on and off, and why, kept on your iPhone so you can see it in Settings.

## In your iCloud Keychain

The date your free nights started is saved in your iCloud Keychain, if you use it, so a new phone continues it. It is only that date. Apple keeps it end-to-end encrypted for your Apple Account, and we never receive it. If iCloud Keychain is off, it stays on your iPhone.

## Purchases

The one-time purchase goes through Apple. We receive no payment details and nothing that identifies you.

## Location and maps

The app asks for location so it can tell whether you are at a block's place while it is closed. If you do not allow it, everything still works: every block runs on its hours, wherever you are.

When you search for a place or look at the map, the search and the map come from Apple Maps, under Apple's privacy policy. Dusk does not see or keep what Apple Maps receives.
